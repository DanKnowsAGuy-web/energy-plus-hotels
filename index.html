What dirty power costs a hotel.

We work on hotels, so we know exactly which loads carry the stress. Single-box vendors never look at most of this. We measure all of it, at the panel, before and after.

  1. Elevator traction motors

    Hard starts at six to eight times running current are the primary cause of early motor failure. Emergency modernization runs $50K to $150K per cab, with four to eight week parts lead times and ADA exposure while the cab is down.

    $50K–$150K per cab

  2. Per-room PTAC fleet

    Your per-room HVAC units run around the clock whether guests are in the room or not, carrying more electrical stress than almost any load in the building. Replacing the fleet across a 150-room property runs $260K to $480K, and dirty power gets you there years too early.

    $260K–$480K per property

  3. Aquatic, spa, and laundry drives

    Pool and spa pump replacement runs $8K to $25K per pump, and a closure in peak season turns directly into guest dissatisfaction and review damage. Commercial laundry drives fail the same way: properties typically run four to eight machines continuously.

    $8K–$25K per pump

None of this appears on your bill as a line item called waste, and none of it shows up in a standard energy audit. That is exactly why it survives for years.
